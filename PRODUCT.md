# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web. Astro public site in `apps/web`, with React islands shared from `src`.
Payload in `apps/cms` supplies overrides for the checked-in referential copy.

## Users

Institutional investors first, then private clients, companies and issuers,
and savers through the Sama Naffa gateway.

## Product Purpose

Explain EVEREST Finance's integrated UEMOA market services, give access to its
analyses, and support entry into a client relationship.

## Positioning

Integrated responsibility from structuring through settlement, demonstrated
through the house's publications rather than unsupported claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

`docs/referentiel/SITEWEB-REFERENTIEL-V2.1.md` is the public-copy authority.
Preserve all words, CMS ids, home block order, server-rendered text, and
keyboard/no-JavaScript access. Native disclosures are permitted.

No invented numbers, customer claims, investment outcomes, or public copy.
The six charter colors and regulated French voice remain binding.

## Brand Commitments

EVEREST Finance, Dakar; mountain and caravan identity; forest and gold charter.
The owner rejects decorative vertical rails, repeated card grids, and arbitrary
typographic treatments. The owner delegates completion of the current redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

Charter-graded summit poster and short film, actual publication PDFs and
rendered covers, EVEREST Finance logo, Sama Naffa logo.

Partner logos, live BRVM data, and house photography are not cleared for this pass.
Seeding the staging CMS requires separate permission every time.
